# Research Discovery Novelty Check

Update the Bet's novelty record in `research/discovery/bets/<bet_id>/BET.json` and its status in `research/discovery/PORTFOLIO.json`. Do not write `IDEA_CANDIDATES.md`, add `rank_score`, write `EXPERIMENT_PLAN.md`, force a winner, or report that prior art by itself refutes the Bet.

Search the axes on which the claim depends: problem setting, theoretical mechanism, mapped variables, intervention or decision, observable prediction, evaluation regime, and known boundary cases. Record reproducible queries, sources read directly, the most dangerous nearest work, exact overlap, exact remaining delta, and coverage gaps. An empty search leaves novelty unresolved; it never proves novelty.

Prior work may invalidate the stable `nearest_work` pre-probe eligibility gate and require reframing, merging, parking, or killing the candidate. It does not become a free-text gate or a failed scientific probe. Only evidence that bears on the stated binding premise may support or refute the scientific idea. Preserve that distinction and keep the strongest defensible claim ceiling explicit.
