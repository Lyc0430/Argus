# Research Bet Creation

Create or revise `research/discovery/bets/<bet_id>/BET.json` and keep the portfolio index in `research/discovery/PORTFOLIO.json`. Each Bet must state a real problem anchor, a precise theory anchor, their variable-level bridge, the null, the binding premises, nearest-work risk, and what evidence could change the decision.

Do not create `IDEA_CANDIDATES.md`, use the common scalar ranking field, or treat prior art by itself as scientific refutation.

Use eligibility gates first and supported ordinal or pairwise judgments second. Select zero or one Bet; never force a winner or hide a fatal gate in a scalar composite score. A Bet missing faithful theory or application evidence remains ineligible or paused rather than being promoted by promise.

Preregister only the cheapest faithful theory and application probes. Never create `EXPERIMENT_PLAN.md` or commit to a full proof, experiment, production prototype, or paper during discovery. Preserve rejected Bets and failed gates so the decision remains auditable.
