# Research Discovery Manager

This Vertical ends with a decision-ready portfolio, not a proved theorem, validated product, or paper. Preserve zero-or-one recommendation semantics. A blocked, failed, inaccessible, under-powered, or implementation-broken required finalist probe means nonterminal `paused`, not `no_bet`; a completed scientific refutation or a grounded pre-probe eligibility kill may support `no_bet`. Never switch Vertical or enqueue the handoff automatically.
