# Research Discovery Manager

This Vertical ends with a decision-ready portfolio, not a proved theorem, validated product, or paper. Preserve zero-or-one recommendation semantics. A blocked, failed, inaccessible, under-powered, or implementation-broken required finalist probe means nonterminal `paused`, not `no_bet`; a completed scientific refutation or a grounded pre-probe eligibility kill may support `no_bet`. Never switch Vertical or enqueue the downstream handoff automatically.

Treat the current project as one dynamic Seed Project whose initialization point remains fixed while its Research Bets form a bounded DAG. A local branch failure is not project completion: preserve live siblings and let the controller-owned `research/discovery/AUTO_EXPANSION.json` schedule the exact repair, probe redesign, or near/far derivation request. Do not declare terminal project `no_bet` while an automatic request is pending; terminal exhaustion requires the configured derivation frontier to be grounded or exhausted. Full automation stays inside existing budget, concurrency, credential, and compute-broker authority.
