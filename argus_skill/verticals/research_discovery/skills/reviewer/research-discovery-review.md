# Research Discovery Review

Review current artifacts independently of the Manager's preference. Apply eligibility gates before supported ordinal or pairwise comparison; never certify a scalar composite score or force a winner.

Check the most dangerous nearest work and exact claimed delta; the problem/theory anchors, variable mapping, dependency claim, prediction, and no-garnish test; whether each minimum probe bears on its premise; and whether execution status, failure class, and idea status remain separate. Reject infrastructure failure as scientific evidence, proxy drift above its claim ceiling, lost nulls or failed attempts, and stale decision or handoff bindings.

Require `recommended` to name exactly one eligible Bet and bind a valid `HANDOFF.json`; require `no_bet` to ground every material rejection and omit a handoff; treat `paused` as nonterminal. Before approval, run the discovery package validator and cite the current lane and decision evidence.
