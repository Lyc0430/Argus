# Research Discovery Method Distillation

Create a project-local reusable discovery Skill only when concrete evidence establishes a durable method gap or repeatable procedure. Distill the trigger, generalized failure, decision rule, smallest reliable procedure, required evidence, applicability limits, and a verification scenario.

Do not store project history, candidate identities, dataset-specific outcomes, handoff state, or generic brainstorming advice in a Skill. Those belong in project artifacts. If the available material is only a narrative or one-off result, create no Skill.
