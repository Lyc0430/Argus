# Research Discovery Method Adaptation

Adapt a reusable discovery method only after a concrete gap is observed. Preserve the source method's trigger and evidence boundary, change only the step the gap invalidated, state the new applicability limits, and verify the adaptation on a representative scenario before keeping it.

Do not create a provisional Skill merely to collect an unspecified gap. Do not copy whole workflows, store candidate identities or project history, or pad the adaptation with generic brainstorming advice. Without a concrete method gap, leave the Skill unchanged and keep project state in project artifacts.
