# Research Discovery Planning

Plan adaptively around the next highest-information move; do not require every discovery method for every Bet. Keep candidate generation separate from selection, and freeze eligibility plus ordinal or pairwise comparison criteria before observing finalist outcomes.

For each finalist, choose the cheapest faithful theory probe and application probe that could change the decision. Parallelize only genuinely independent searches or probes. Record blocked access, infrastructure, or execution as `untested` or `inconclusive`, never as negative evidence about the idea.
